    Gold making charge

    Personal and business customers each have their own charge. Enter a percentage of the bar’s gold value or a flat amount per bar; change either box and the other follows at today’s rate. Enter 0 for none.

    Silver making charge

    Personal and business customers each have their own charge. Enter a percentage of the product’s silver value or a flat amount per piece; change either box and the other follows at today’s rate. Enter 0 for none.
